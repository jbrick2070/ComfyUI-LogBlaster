LogBlaster quick guide

Watch ComfyUI logs live. Try TEST BLAST for a short demo. Scroll up to hold history; press Space to resume.

1–4
Choose CRT, CYBER, SCOPE or ARCADE shaders
V
Cycle CASCADE (large live line), DUAL (hero + history), DRUM (curved history), FLAT (uniform text)
T / Y
Cycle tint / toggle Comfy black and yellow
/ / Escape
Focus filter / clear filter while typing
E / Space
Jump to errors / pause or resume scrolling
− / +
Adjust text size; MORE also contains COPY
M / A
Toggle synth / capture system or tab audio
Shift+A
Capture microphone (also Shift+Click AUDIO)
B / F
Test blast / fullscreen
VR
With a tethered headset (Quest Link or Air Link), the VR button puts the log on a curved wall around you. Trigger: next shader. Grip: next view. Thumbstick left or right: narrower or wider wall; up or down: text size
? / H / Escape
Open or close help / close this guide

Audio: SYS and MIC are silent analyser taps: zero echo. FILE (drop an audio file) and SYNTH play through your speakers. Click AUDIO again to stop its input.

Your system’s reduced-motion setting caps FX at 60% and disables moving distortions and animated scrolling. The FX slider can lower it further.
